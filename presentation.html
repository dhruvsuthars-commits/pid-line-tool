Market Challenge

The Bottleneck in P&ID Line List Management

Traditional Manual Process

  • Tedious Manual Entry: Engineers spend 20+ hours reading P&ID drawings and typing line tags.
  • Inconsistent Line Conventions: Projects use conflicting line formats (Fluid-Seq-Size vs Size-Fluid-Class).
  • High Error Rates: Typographical errors in line size or pipe class trigger expensive downstream piping re-work.

Automated Solution

  • Instant Automated Parsing: Segregates Fluid Code, Sequence No, Size, Class & Insulation instantly.
  • Universal AI Adaptability: Simply type plain-English philosophy rules to handle any client standard.
  • Standardized Output: Direct population of official Linelist_reference master templates.
